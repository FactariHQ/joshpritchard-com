What I do

Three things, and they are the same thing

Behavior analysis is a science of what people actually do. That applies to a four-year-old learning to ask for help, and it applies to a company of two hundred people trying to hold a clinical standard.

Build

Companies that deliver the science

Factari, an infrastructure venture for behavioral-science entrepreneurs, and Adventure Child Therapy, an ABA provider operating in Colorado and Oklahoma. Before those, JKP Analysts and Lodestone Academy.

For clinical teams

One more set of eyes on a stuck case

The Quick Review Consult is a short outside observation — clinic, home, or school — written up and handed to the consulting team who own the behavior plan. I do not take the case and I do not write the plan. Your clinicians get something concrete to build from.

The book

Edited by Byron Wine and Joshua K. Pritchard · 2018 · Hedgehog Publishers, Orlando · ISBN 978-0-9995467-1-0

A working reference for the field, assembled because the OBM literature was scattered across journals that practitioners do not read. Reviewed in Perspectives on Behavior Science in 2019.

If you own one

You are allowed to just ask someone

Owning a clinical practice is a lonely job with a small number of people who genuinely understand it. I have built four companies, sold one, turned around others, and sat on both sides of an acquisition table. If you are trying to decide whether to grow, partner, hire, restructure, or get out, I am happy to be the person you think out loud with.

There is no pitch attached to that conversation and nothing about it obligates you to anything.
